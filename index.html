Netix Shield Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Netix Shield is a fraud and abuse detection app for Shopify stores, operated by Netix Recovery ("Netix", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information Netix Shield accesses when a merchant installs it, how we use that information, and the rights merchants and their customers have.

1. What Netix Shield does

Netix Shield reviews a store's recent orders, refunds, returns, discounts and payment disputes to flag patterns that may indicate repeat refund abuse, payment manipulation, policy exploitation, friendly fraud or chargeback fraud. It also lets a merchant look up a single customer or order and generate a case report with a timeline and risk indicators.

2. Information we access

When you install the app and approve its permissions, Shopify lets Netix Shield read the following from your store:

DataExamplesWhy we need it
OrdersOrder number, date, totals, discount amounts and codes, fulfilment and delivery statusTo detect abusive refund, discount and delivery-claim patterns
CustomersCustomer name and email address attached to ordersTo group a customer's orders and show which customer a flag relates to
Refunds and returnsRefund amounts and dates, return statusTo measure refund and return behaviour
Payment disputesDispute records from Shopify Payments, where availableTo detect chargeback fraud
Store connection detailsYour shop domain and the access token Shopify issues to the appTo sign in to your store and make the requests above

We do not access customers' payment card numbers or full payment details.

3. How we use information

We use this information only to provide Netix Shield to the merchant who installed it: running scans, showing results, building investigation reports and handling the app's billing through Shopify. We do not sell personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers needed to run the app (for example, our hosting provider), who may only process it on our behalf.

4. What we store and for how long

  • Store connection record: your shop domain and access token are stored so the app can keep working. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Scan results and investigation reports: these are generated on request from your Shopify data and shown to you. We do not save them on our servers. If you print or save a report as a PDF, that file is created in your own browser.
  • Server logs: basic technical logs (such as request times, shop domain and error messages) may be kept for troubleshooting and security.

5. Automated flags are indicators, not verdicts

Netix Shield applies fixed rules to your store's data and shows the results to you. A flag or risk level is an indicator for you to review, not a finding of wrongdoing. Decisions about any customer remain yours; the app does not block, refund or contact customers on its own.

6. Security

Data moves between Shopify, the app and your browser over encrypted connections. Access to the app is limited to authenticated Shopify sessions, and we only request the permissions the app needs.

7. Rights of merchants and customers

Merchants control the data in their Shopify store. Customers whose details appear in a store's orders can ask the merchant, or us, to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about them. We honour Shopify's mandatory data-request and data-erasure requests, and we respond to direct requests within 30 days. Because we do not keep copies of order or customer data, a deletion request mainly concerns the store connection record and logs described above.

We handle personal information in line with applicable data protection laws, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and, where they apply, the GDPR and the CCPA.

8. International processing

Our hosting provider's servers may be located outside your country. Where that happens we rely on appropriate safeguards so your data is protected to the standard this policy describes.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify merchants through the app or by email.
